# Design risk register

| ID | Risk | Current control | Closure evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| R-01 | Exact WTS probe quote exceeds CNY 180 | Common cartridge accepts a substitute 1/2- or 3/4-inch NPT K=1 probe | Delivered quote and updated BOM remain below US$150 |
| R-02 | WTS probe is not electrically compatible with the Your Cee board | Wet mechanics and dry board tray are independent; kit probe remains a diagnostic spare | Stable two-standard calibration with the WTS cell |
| R-03 | PH8012 seller supplies a multiwire variant rather than BNC | Procurement requires BNC/Q9 photo and exact variant statement | Received connector photo and pH 4/7 bench calibration |
| R-04 | Seller geometry differs from the published drawing | Conservative proxies and replaceable inserts; all final fits are parameterized | Caliper sheet committed before final CAD |
| R-05 | Printed wet block leaks at cartridge pilots | Purchased threads, O-ring cartridge interface, 4 mm wet walls, hard compression stop | Thirty-minute static leak test plus ten pump cycles with dry witness paper |
| R-06 | Dosing tube bundle tips or drags the float | Structural tether eye and 1 N lateral-load heel check | Physical heel test keeps every dry seal above water |
| R-07 | Long probes strike the reservoir or are damaged during handling | Removable polymer guard with rounded bottom and 20 mm tip clearance | Drop/handling mock-up and installed reservoir-clearance check |
| R-08 | Battery hatch compromises the primary dry seal | Independent top hatch with its own gasket; main tub stays closed during charging | Splash test after ten battery-removal cycles |
| R-09 | FDM shrinkage prevents cartridge service | 0.30 mm-per-side prototype clearance and a small fit coupon before the full print | Cartridge installs by hand and has no visible rocking after screws are tightened |
| R-10 | Center of mass or tube load reduces freeboard | Battery/pump/valves low and centered; parameterized ballast pockets | Fusion mass study followed by physical displacement and 100 g corner-load tests |

Chemical compatibility remains a later operational risk, but it is not a shell-design freeze gate for this iteration.
